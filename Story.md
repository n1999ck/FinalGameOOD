Thanatos Embalming, inc.
January 26, 20XX
1:38 PM


The pavement is cracked. Seven parking spots line the rectangle. 
They hold the Ciera, an Acura MDX, a Ford Expedition, a Toyota Prius, and a postal service delivery van.
The empty spot usually holds a collection van.

A 1985 Oldsmobile Cutlass Ciera rolls into the Thanatos Embalming parking lot, coming to a stop in its usual spot.
A greying man gets out, wearing a jacket over a flannel shirt. Anthony Boone shivers as he locks his truck door.
"Hoo wee it's cold out here. I hope the ladies are doing alright on the pickup."

The ladies - his coworkers - aren't here. They've just left on a collection call. 
A call had come in about a bad car accident, seemingly caused by the icy roads. Multiple deaths occurred at the scene.
That's where Thanatos comes in. Everyone needs a caretaker in the end, and Anthony was honored to do it.

Anthony had planned to take a day off today, but it seemed that the pickup was going to be a nasty one. 
His coworkers insisted that he stay home and relax. Angel, the newer girl, wouldn't even tell him the location.
"They might need some help in the embalming room. I'll just go over to the office before they get back."

Proud of his sneaky plan, Anthony enters the front door. The lobby doesn't feel much warmer than the wind outside.
"Hey there, Miss Am--" He begins to greet his manager, before looking up from his gloves and realizing that he was alone in the room.
Amelia Balmer, owner and operator of the Thanatos franchise, usually sat at the reception desk. She's nowhere in sight.

"Huh... I guess she's back in the break room."
Anthony stands still for a moment, his glove removal half-finished. 
Then, he crosses the lobby to the employees-only door and lets himself into the back hallway.

The back hallway is a little dingy, Anthony has to admit. A faint smell of formaldehyde reaches the air here.
He walks to the end, peeking into the open doors to his right. 
The office, break room, and locker room are empty.

"Her car was out front," Anthony mutters to himself.
The only rooms left are the embalming rooms, and it'd be strange for Amelia in there.
Usually she avoids them entirely. Anthony looks around uneasily, listening... nothing.

"Something's not right. And wasn't there a delivery van out there?"
He turns to the embalming room 2 door. Before entering, Anthony usually puts on a suit of PPE.
Right now, though, he elects to skip changing. There's not anyone on the tables right now, anyway.

Anthony pushes open the door and steps into the embalming room. The floor is clean, the tables empty.
The large refrigerator hums beside him, speaking for the dead within. Besides them, he's alone.
But alongside the formaldehyde is another familiar smell. It's a little different from usual, but Anthony knows blood when he smells it.

"Miss Amelia? Hello?" Anthony is looking around frantically now. 
He stumbles to the double door on his left and pushes it open.
The smell of blood increases tenfold.

Amelia Balmer lies facedown on the ground before him, a stagnant pool of blood surrounding.
"Oh shit..." Anthony knows death when he sees it, and it's unmistakable that Amelia is dead.
A trocar sits in the pool beside her, its sharp tip coated in the blood.

Reeling, Anthony looks around, realizing that the doors to the receiving bay are already open.
Another man lies there, dropped box to his side. Anthony runs over - he's still breathing.
So here's the delivery man.
